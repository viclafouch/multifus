# Multifus

Multifus brings the Dofus Retro window of the right character in front, and
lays windows of its own over the game.

## Language

### Windows

**Client**:
One running Dofus Retro window, with one character logged in.
_Avoid_: instance

**In front**:
The client on top of every other Dofus window, the one the player is looking
at. On screen: « au premier plan ».
_Avoid_: focused

**Switch**:
Bringing a client in front, whatever asked for it.
_Avoid_: bascule, which names a toggle in this app

**Holding the keyboard**:
Receiving what the player types. The client in front holds it, except while
the Notes do.
_Avoid_: focus, foreground

**In the game**:
The state where a client is in front, or where the Notes hold the keyboard.
Shortcuts act only in the game.

### Overlays

**Overlay**:
A window of Multifus laid over the game: the rune table, the character wheel,
the banner and the Notes. The Notes are the only one that takes the keyboard.

**Notes**:
The overlay where the player writes, shared by every character. It stays where
the player put it on the screen, above every application, where the rune table
is posted on one client. On screen: « Notes » in French and English, « Notas »
in Spanish.
_Avoid_: bloc-notes, notepad, memo

**Note**:
What the player wrote in the Notes, kept across restarts. The Notes hold one
note.
_Avoid_: text, which belongs to the quick text, page

### Releases

**Patch notes**:
The changes a version of Multifus brings, offered after an update. On screen:
« patch note ».
_Avoid_: notes, which belongs to the Notes
