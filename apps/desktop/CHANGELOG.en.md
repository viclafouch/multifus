# Patch notes

## 0.1.0 - 2026-09-23

### The first version

- Multifus brings the window of the character whose turn it is in front of you on Dofus Retro. Free, no account, no ads, and the code is public.
- Mac and Windows on day one, in French, English and Spanish.
- The next updates install from the application, in one click.

### AutoFocus

- The window of the character whose turn it is comes forward as the turn starts.
- Trade, party, guild, private message, challenge, craft, Perceptor attacked: you choose the triggers.
- A character sitting in the Dock or the taskbar comes back in front of you.

### Character wheel

- You hold a shortcut, the wheel opens in the middle of the screen, you aim at a head and you let go.

### Quick move

- You switch Quick move on, you click where you want to go, and the next character lands in front of you.

### Rune table

- The rune weight table lies over the game window, on the key you chose.

### Private messages

- Your Dofus Retro private messages reach your phone through Telegram, within the second. You also learn which character has just been disconnected.

### Quick texts

- You store a sentence under a key combination. In the game, you press it, and the sentence is written in your chat field.

### Mac and Windows

- On Mac, an application checked by Apple, with no right-click and no security setting to work around, on Intel and Apple Silicon Macs alike.
- On Windows 10 and 11, every character takes its own taskbar button, with its nickname and its class head.
